FOOTBALL STRATEGIES ON POLYMARKET — RESEARCHED RULEBOOKS
=========================================================
Compiled 2026-09-01 from published studies, quant write-ups and our own
2025 draw-at-60 backtest (backend/backtest/football.py). Documented edges
are 2-5c per $1, so the fee/slippage arithmetic decides survival — every
strategy here is backtestable on our existing pipeline before risking a cent.


STRATEGY 1 — FAVORITE-LONGSHOT BIAS
-----------------------------------
Universe: Polymarket soccer moneylines, volume above $10k.

Rules:
  1. Never buy any YES priced under 20c.
  2. Buy the favorite's YES only when priced 60-85c.
  3. Buy NO on any outcome priced 5-15c ONLY IF Pinnacle's de-vigged
     probability for that outcome is at least 3c lower than the Polymarket
     price (the sharp book is the filter — no guessing).
  4. Limit orders only; skip if the spread is wider than 2c.

Exit: hold to settlement. No in-play management.
Size: flat stake, equal per trade. Judge after 100+ trades, never sooner.

References:
  - QuantPedia — Systematic Edges in Prediction Markets
    https://quantpedia.com/systematic-edges-in-prediction-markets/
  - The Polymarket Quant Playbook (0xIcaruss)
    https://medium.com/@0xicaruss/the-polymarket-quant-playbook-a-data-driven-framework-for-extracting-edge-in-prediction-markets-d9744d90477d

STRATEGY 2 — BACK THE DRAW
--------------------------
Universe: evenly-matched games, defined mechanically: both win prices
within 10c of each other pre-kickoff. (The Soccermatics version restricts
further to EPL top-6 vs top-6.)

Rules — pre-kickoff:
  1. Buy Draw when priced below 27c in a qualifying game.
  2. Draw at 27c or higher -> no bet.

Rules — in-play (supported by our own 2025 backtest, 70'/75' rows):
  1. Game still level at the 70th minute -> buy Draw at market.
  2. A goal is scored after entry -> sell immediately, take the loss.
  3. Otherwise hold to settlement.

Size: flat stake; ~3 of 4 pre-kickoff bets lose — the 3-4x payouts carry
the strategy, so never increase stake after losses.

References:

  - Our own draw-at-60 2025 study (Backtesting page, Football section)


STRATEGY 3 — MODEL-VS-ODDS VALUE BETTING (POISSON)
--------------------------------------------------
Universe: leagues with clean stats (big five + UCL via api-football).

Rules:
  1. Expected goals per team = team's average goals scored x opponent's
     average goals conceded / league average; multiply the home side by
     the home factor (~1.2).
  2. Convert the two expected-goal numbers into win/draw/lose
     probabilities with a Poisson table (fixed formula, no judgment).
  3. Buy an outcome only when model probability - Polymarket price >= 5c.
  4. Gap under 5c -> no bet. Zero bets most days is the system working.

Exit: hold to settlement.
Size: flat stake, cap any single game at 2% of bankroll.

References:
  - Bivariate Poisson profitable-betting study
    https://www.sciencepublishinggroup.com/article/10.11648/j.ajtas.20150403.15
  - The Market for EPL Odds (arXiv)
    https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.03614
  - Machine Learning in Sports Betting — systematic review (arXiv)
    https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.21484


STRATEGY 4 — CLOSING-LINE VALUE (PINNACLE FILTER)
-------------------------------------------------
Universe: any Polymarket soccer game Pinnacle also prices.

Rules:
  1. Within 1 hour of kickoff, take Pinnacle's three odds; convert each to
     a probability (1/odds); divide each by their sum -> the FAIR price
     (removes Pinnacle's ~2-4% margin).
  2. Buy any Polymarket outcome priced 4c or more below its fair price.
  3. Gap under 4c -> no bet.

Exit: hold to settlement, or sell when Polymarket converges to within 1c
of the Pinnacle fair price.

Second use — the grader: log every bet from strategies 1-3 against the
Pinnacle closing fair price. A strategy whose entries beat the close on
average is good even during losing weeks; one that does not beat the
close gets switched off regardless of recent profit.
