## LIMITATIONS — measured, cite these when scoping

### 1. Closed trades MUST be reconstructed from the trade log
`/positions` is a HOLDINGS ledger. A market sold out entirely VANISHES from it.
Proven on a real wallet:
    fully-exited assets PRESENT in /positions : 1
    fully-exited assets MISSING from /positions: 6
      MISSING: Cincinnati Reds vs. Pittsburgh Pirates
      MISSING: Chicago White Sox vs. New York Yankees
Only 67 of 564 positions carried a non-zero realizedPnl.
The client SELLS BEFORE RESOLUTION as a habit ("usually I sell it before the
final outcome"), so this is his normal case, not an edge case.
=> Realized P/L, Win Rate, Holding Time, Average-down and the whole Closed
   Trades table come from matching SELLs to BUYs per asset in `/trades`.
   There is NO shortcut, from the free API or the paid one.
   Use WEIGHTED-AVERAGE cost per market, not FIFO — simpler to explain and it
   matches the `avgPrice` Polymarket itself shows. Must support partial exits.
   REDEEM and MERGE in `/activity` are NOT sells: a winner held to the end
   settles at 100c via REDEEM. Miss it and win rate is silently wrong.

### 2. `/trades` is capped at 10,000 fills per wallet
    offset=10000 -> 0 results
    offset=20000 -> HTTP 400 {"error":"max historical trades offset of 10000 exceeded"}
Older fills are unreachable. Fine for the client's 3 personal accounts today,
but STORE TRADES FROM DAY ONE so our own depth accumulates past the ceiling.

### 3. "Max price after exit" / "lowest after entry" — resolution
`fidelity` is in MINUTES; `interval=max` is capped at 10-minute buckets:

| interval | fidelity | points | median gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| max | 1 or 10 | 81 | 600s |
| 1d | 1 | 796 | 60s |
| 6h | 1 | 361 | 60s |
| 1h | 1 | 61 | 60s |

1-minute for the last ~24h, 10-minute beyond. A spike inside a 10-minute bucket
is invisible — and catching spikes is the entire point of the metric.
RETROACTIVELY UNFIXABLE for past trades in markets we never tracked.
OUR EDGE: for markets already tracked in this app we hold 5s ticks, 1s for live
MLB. Read our `ticks` table when we have it, fall back to prices-history, and
SAY WHICH IN THE UI. This is the one place we beat the paid product.

### 4. Profile NAME lookup is unreliable
`/v1/leaderboard?userName=` searches the RANKED leaderboard only:
"swisstony" -> 1 hit, "theo" -> 3 hits, a nonsense name -> 0.
A low-volume account (exactly what the brother/friend may be) may not be
indexed. Many wallets have no display name at all — the client's own example
returns the default `0x2c33…-1759935795465`.
=> Parse `0x[0-9a-f]{40}` out of a pasted URL or address; that always works.
   Offer name search as a convenience only. Do not promise it.

### 5. Deposits / Withdrawals / Cash balance are ON-CHAIN only
Not in any Polymarket API (see the 404 list). They are USDC transfers to/from
the proxy wallet — Goldsky subgraph or Polygonscan. A second data source, ~a
day. LEAVE OUT OF V1.

### 6. Fees
Polymarket charges per-share fees and the API doesn't document whether its PnL
fields are gross or net. Reconcile against the client's own profile before
trusting any P/L number.

## RIDE-TO-ZERO LOSSES VANISH FROM /positions — FIXED Aug 6 (client caught it)

Client: "it shows all the wins and none of my losses." Correct, and the cause
is an API behaviour: Polymarket DROPS resolved-lost holdings from /positions
after a while (his test wallet: 454 net-long assets absent). Wins never hid —
they close via REDEEM. So the engine only ever saw sold losses.
FIX: residual net-long trips absent from /positions ("ghosts") resolve via
CLOB /markets/{condition_id} — closed flag + per-outcome WINNER booleans (the
one reliable source; gamma's condition_ids filter returns nothing). Winner ->
close at $1 (old win redeemed beyond the 500-activity window), loser -> $0.
Closed resolutions cache forever (immutable); unknown retries after 600s;
first pass = hundreds of lookups behind a semaphore(8), ~5s, then cached.
Also widened: /positions rows with curPrice==0 count as dead REGARDLESS of
redeemable (dead-but-redeemable losers exist — the "Redeemed 0" cleanups).
His test account: 286 closed/65.4% "win rate" -> 536 closed/45.7% honest.
NOTE: trips neither in /positions nor CLOB-closed stay open (conservative) —
they resolve on later views as markets close.

## FEES — SUPERSEDED Aug 7: EXACT FEES FROM CASH FLOWS, NOT THE SCHEDULE

THE SCHEDULE BELOW IS NOW ONLY A FALLBACK. Client compared the tracker to his
profile and the numbers were off. Root cause chain (all verified Aug 7):
1. Polymarket's PROFILE P/L chart (user-pnl-api.polymarket.com/user-pnl)
   IGNORES FEES ENTIRELY — it is price*size flows only. Fees are real cash
   (activity usdcSize proves it) but the profile never shows them.
2. Our schedule OVERCHARGED history: sports taker fee was 3% before mid-2026,
   5% after. Every pre-change fill was billed at 5/3 the real fee (client's
   account: we said $1,045, reality $822).
3. FIX: data-api /activity rows carry usdcSize = the USDC that ACTUALLY
   moved. fee = usdcSize - price*size for buys, price*size - usdcSize for
   sells; makers diff to ~0. dataapi.fetch_fill_fees builds the map keyed
   (tx, side, round(price,6), round(size,4)); sync_account uses it and
   store.update_fees RE-STAMPS stored fills every sync (self-healing, all
   accounts). Schedule = fallback for fills beyond the activity window only.
4. DISPLAY (user decision): headline Realized/Total P/L and closed-row P/L
   are BEFORE fees so they reconcile with the profile page the client stares
   at; the fee-included figure sits in parentheses underneath. WIN/LOSS and
   win rate STAY net-of-fees (settled business rule unchanged).
5. Reconciliation check: gross total tracked within ~$15 of user-pnl on a
   quiet book; expect drift while open positions move (mark timing).
GOTCHA (FIXED Aug 7 evening, commit 51cdb6c): the resolution cache was
in-memory only, so EVERY deploy re-fired the ~200-market CLOB storm;
rate-limited lookups cached as unknown and resolved-zero losses silently
left the closed list (Testing read 539 closed/+$1005 realized instead of
~730/−$350 — caught by the verify pass comparing gross vs user-pnl). Now
persisted in trader_resolutions (a closed market is final: survives
restarts, storm happens once ever); failures log a warning.
ALSO Aug 7 (commit 9d0478d): /activity AGGREGATES partial fills that
/trades splits, so ~5% of fills missed the exact-fee key match and kept the
schedule fallback ($141 overstated on testmlb). A transaction's leftover
cash fee is now apportioned across its unmatched fills weighted by
size*p*(1−p) (service._assign_fees). Verified: every key-matched fill
agrees with the cash-implied fee TO THE CENT on all three accounts.

## FEES — the old flat schedule (docs, verified Aug 6 — FALLBACK ONLY now)

    fee = shares x feeRate x price x (1 - price)      [price in 0..1]

- TAKERS ONLY. Makers are never charged.
- feeRate by category: Crypto 0.07 | Sports/Economics/Culture/Weather/Other
  0.05 | Finance/Politics/Mentions/Tech 0.04 | Geopolitics 0.
- Symmetric curve peaking at 50c: 100 crypto shares @50c = $1.75 (verified
  against their own example). Rounded to 5dp, min charge 0.00001 USDC.
- WORKED EXAMPLES for his typical MLB (Sports, 0.05) trades — CORRECTED Aug 6
  (first version had halved arithmetic):
    buy 500 @20c (taker): 500 x 0.05 x 0.2 x 0.8  = $4.00  (4.0% of stake)
    sell 500 @25c (taker): 500 x 0.05 x 0.25 x 0.75 = $4.69 (3.75% of stake)
    taker-both-ways round trip: $8.69 on $25 gross = ~35% of a thin scalp.
  MAKERS PAY ZERO (all three sources). A resting limit exit = $0 fee, so his
  realistic round trip is ~$4.00 (=16%); maker both ways = $0. As % of stake
  the fee is 0.05 x (1-p): HIGHER on cheap longshots (4% @20c vs 2.5% @50c) —
  the curve taxes his exact habitat hardest.
  => MAKER/TAKER IS DETECTABLE from the public API (verified Aug 6): data-api
  /trades has a takerOnly param (defaults TRUE). Fetch both feeds and diff:
  a fill present only under takerOnly=false is a MAKER fill (fee $0); present
  in the default feed = TAKER (formula applies). Match rows on
  transactionHash+asset+price+size; the rare ambiguous duplicate falls back to
  taker (conservative). Ground truth if ever needed: the on-chain OrderFilled
  event behind each transactionHash records the ACTUAL fee paid (needs an RPC;
  reconciliation only, not a dependency). Sample whale was ~94% maker.
  => Store the maker/taker tag per fill from day one — the diff only works
  within the 10k-offset window, so classify at ingest time.
  => BACKTESTER (when resumed): fee model needs a maker/taker flag PER LEG —
  it is the difference between 35% and 16% drag on the same strategy. Noted
  cross-reference in V2-BACKTESTING.md execution-realism section.
- HOW WE APPLY IT: fee computed per FILL at matching time from the market's
  category (events.category, already stored); round-trip P/L = proceeds -
  cost - entry fees - exit fees; WIN = net > 0 per the settled definition.
- ASSUME TAKER ON EVERY FILL: data-api trade rows carry NO maker/taker/fee
  field (checked). He market-buys panicky prices = taker behaviour; the
  assumption is conservative (worst case slightly overstates fees).
  Reconcile once against his Polymarket profile before trusting the cards.
- DO NOT use CLOB market maker_base_fee/taker_base_fee (both read 1000 on a
  live MLB market while docs say makers pay nothing — clearly not the live
  schedule). Category rates from the docs are authoritative; keep them in ONE
  config constant so a Polymarket fee change is a one-line update.
- SPREAD: nothing to add for the accounts tracker — /trades prices are the
  ACTUAL executed fills, spread already baked in. (Spread modelling matters
  only in the backtester, where fills are simulated from midpoints.)
