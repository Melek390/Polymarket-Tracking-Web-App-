## FEED LAG, PROPERLY MEASURED + PER-MOVE ATTRIBUTION (supersedes July 31)

The July 31 cross-correlation is REPLACED. Measuring against real 1s data
(PHI@BAL, market 180) showed a reaction is not an instant — it bleeds in over
~10s — and that the lead scales with how OBVIOUS the play is:
    Home Run 17-23s early | Triple 7.7s | Double 2.8s | routine 2-4s
    median across all reactions 4.0s (our estimator says 4.0s — it agrees)
frontend/src/mlbLag.js now:
- reactions(): group consecutive moves (>=1c, gap <=4s) into bursts w/ centroid
- estimateLag() -> {lagMs = median offset of a burst CENTRE, leadMs = median
  START offset of the BIG bursts (top quartile, >=3c), samples, ...}. Needs >=5
  matched bursts (within -8s..+30s of a play stamp) or returns null.
- attributeMoves() -> every burst + the play that caused it + that burst's lead
- moveAt(attributed, ts) -> the move under the cursor
Tooltip names the cause: "price moved 68.0c — caused by Triple · 2 RBI", the
description, and "market moved 7.7s before MLB recorded it". Big attributed
moves get a ReferenceLine (green if the play scored). Readout: "≈4.0s typical ·
big moves start ~9s before the play is recorded (from N reactions)".
The -0.5s/+0.5s/auto nudges are GONE (lagOverride removed entirely).
WHY the old one said 3.5s: it anchored on play.end with a 2.5s window, so it
only ever saw the densest slice, not the leading edge.
NOTE: 5s sampling was NOT the limitation — live MLB markets poll at 1s
(verified 12,244 one-second ticks on market 180).

## MLB FEED LAG CORRECTION (July 31) — SUPERSEDED by the Aug 1 section above
## (algorithm replaced; the nudge buttons and lagOverride no longer exist)

Client's insight: the market reacts the INSTANT a play happens; the MLB API
only records it ~5-10s later. So the chart tooltip was blaming a price move on
the PREVIOUS play. He asked us to measure the delay and account for it.
- frontend/src/mlbLag.js estimateLag(ticks, plays, outcomes): cross-correlates
  total |price movement| against play timestamps FOR THAT GAME. 250ms movement
  histogram + prefix sums (O(1) window), scores shifts 0..25s @500ms, returns
  the best. Returns null unless best beats the mean by >=15% (MIN_CONFIDENCE
  1.15) so it never guesses on flat/noisy prices.
- PriceChart: stateAt(plays, ts, lagMs) matches `p.start - lagMs <= ts`.
  Footer shows "MLB feed lag: X.Xs (measured from this game — N price moves)"
  with -0.5s/+0.5s nudges + "auto" reset (lagOverride state).
- VALIDATED with a synthetic-game harness: recovers 0/5/8/12s exactly, and
  refuses on noise. (Test was throwaway; re-create if changing the algorithm.)

## THE BIG PERF ROOT CAUSE (July 27) — SSL context per request, FIXED

THIS was behind the recurring "heavy loading / crashes / pagination broken".
A py-spy dump of the live worker showed the event loop stuck in
ssl.create_default_context() inside httpx.AsyncClient.__init__, called from
mlb linescore_state; the sync markets handler's threadpool sat idle.
Every MLB/CLOB/Gamma call did `async with httpx.AsyncClient(...)` = a NEW
client per call, and each one loads+parses the whole system CA bundle from
disk SYNCHRONOUSLY, blocking the loop. At ~3 MLB calls/s the loop was jammed,
so /api/screener/markets timed out at 8-12s while CPU was ~idle (load 0.5-0.7).
NOT the VM specs, NOT two viewers.
FIX: one shared module-level httpx.AsyncClient in mlb/client.py, polymarket/
clob.py and polymarket/gamma.py (lazy `_http()`, recreated if closed) -> one
SSL context + keep-alive pooling. Verified: markets 8-10s -> 0.013s, loop idle.
LESSON: never construct an httpx client per call on a hot path.

## Price semantics (client asked, investigated in depth)

- Gamma `outcomePrices` == the MIDPOINT, exactly (bid+ask)/2. Verified.
  That is what the screener shows.
- Polymarket's sports "Games" view buttons show the ASK (buy price); their
  market detail "% chance" is midpoint-style.
- On liquid markets spread ~0.1c so they look identical. On illiquid ones
  (e.g. Bolivian league: bid 10c / ask 68c) mid and ask differ by 30+
  points — that was the client's reported "price mismatch". NOT a bug.
- Our tick collector stores CLOB midpoints, so keeping midpoint in the
  screener keeps screener and history consistent. RECOMMENDED (not built):
  add an illiquidity/spread flag on rows with wide bid-ask. Client has not
  chosen yet between: keep mid + flag / switch to ask.
- Also seen: Polymarket postpones fixtures (slug keeps old date,
  gameStartTime updates) — our kickoff follows gameStartTime, correct.
